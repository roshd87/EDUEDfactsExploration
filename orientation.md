## What the repo is

Produces EDPass-submittable EDFacts files from an Ed-Fi + EDU/Stadium warehouse (`edu_wh` dbt marts).
The deliverable is one dbt extension package, `edu_ext_edfacts/`. Everything else in the repo is
spec intake (frozen federal docs → extractions → seeds) and reading material (CEDS Generate wiki,
Obsidian vault of the specs). Split out of GenerateExplorer on 2026-10-12; that repo is gone.

## Conventions and traps

- Generated artifacts (`seeds/`, `wiki/edfacts/`, `tasks/reports/`) are rebuilt byte-identically;
  fix the generator, never the output.
- Frozen docs are on disk only. A fresh clone cannot rebuild the vault or seeds until the fetch and
  freeze are re-run (and ed.gov blocks every non-browser client).
- Tracker: `.planning/` is the issue tracker (`docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`). Commit scopes carry
  plan ids. No `.planning/phases/` directory exists yet; phases live only as `ROADMAP.md` rows and
  `STATE.md` lines.
- Specs are SY 2026-27 (all Inactive at freeze); BRSI and Workbook are SY 2025-26 because no newer
  ones existed. Refresh when files go Active.
- Remote: public snapshot at github.com/roshd87/EDUEDfactsExploration; local `main` history is richer
  and includes git-ignored material in older commits.
