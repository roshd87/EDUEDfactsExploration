# EDFacts Reference Artifacts — SY 2025-26

This directory preserves the official U.S. Department of Education sources frozen for the
SY 2025-26 evidence baseline. Originals are retained without transformation; their provenance and
SHA-256 checksums are in [MANIFEST.csv](MANIFEST.csv).

The [file-specifications index](file-specifications/index.html) is the authoritative local catalog
for EDPass active, inactive, and optional status. All **95 unique** linked DOCX specifications are
preserved in [file-specifications](file-specifications/), with their source URL, version, status,
and SHA-256 in the manifest and [catalog inventory](file-specifications/CATALOG-INVENTORY.tsv).
The set contains 18 Active, 75 Inactive, and 2 Optional (currently inactive) specifications. The
optional FS224 and FS225 links are stored once each and classified as Optional, not double-counted
as Inactive.

Contents currently cached:

- the SY 2025-26 file-specification catalog index;
- all 95 catalog-linked SY 2025-26 file-specification DOCX originals;
- the Business Rules Single Inventory (BRSI) v1.03 and its official index;
- the SY 2025-26 EDFacts workbook with FAQs and its official index; and
- four SY 2025-26 EDPass metadata guides plus their official index.

These artifacts provide catalog status, layouts, category sets, permitted values, validation-rule
inventory, operational guidance, and collection-specific metadata.
